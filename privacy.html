/cutipie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the /cutipie Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and stores information.

Information We May Collect

The Bot may process information provided by Discord, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server IDs and channel IDs
  • Message IDs and message content when required for bot features
  • Moderation actions such as warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans
  • Command usage and bot configuration
  • Server activity information used for statistics, levels, and leaderboards

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate bot features
  • Perform moderation and anti-abuse functions
  • Maintain server settings and configurations
  • Provide levels, statistics, leaderboards, and activity features
  • Diagnose errors and improve the Bot
  • Prevent spam, raids, abuse, and malicious activity

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to host or operate the Bot when necessary.

Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, provide its features, maintain moderation records, or meet security requirements.

Data Removal

Users or Discord server administrators may request deletion of applicable stored data by contacting the Bot operator.

Discord

/cutipie is an independent Discord bot and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Discord Inc.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the /cutipie bot owner through the official /cutipie Discord server.
